## About (company description)

FintiQ Solutions builds secure, scalable fintech platforms for regulated institutions.

We work with banks, financial institutions, and fintech companies on enterprise solutions for cards, corporate banking, payment gateway, cash management, remittance, loan origination (LOS), and loan management (LMS) — plus channel layers for agent, consumer, wallet, merchant, and corporate journeys.

We operate as a technology development partner. Licensed institutions remain the system of record for funds and regulated decisions.

Delivery is UAT-led: done means a live payment, transfer, card, or loan journey signed by your operators. We also provide AI solutions for systems — agentic delivery on the build side, and practical automation inside platforms — with humans accountable for release.
